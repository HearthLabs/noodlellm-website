Common questions

Is there a limit on how much I can use it?
No. It runs on your own computer, so there is no charge per message and no daily cap. Use it as much as you like.
Does anything I type get sent to you?
No. Your questions, answers and files never leave your computer. The app only goes online to download models and check your subscription. The one exception is web search, which is off unless you turn it on: then the question you choose to search with goes to the search service Tavily, under your own account, and never to us.
What happens after the 14 days?
The app pauses until you subscribe. Your models and conversations stay on your computer, and nothing is deleted.
Can I use it on more than one computer?
Yes, on up to 2 of your computers. On a new one, open Account and use the email you paid with; we send you a 6-digit code. At the limit, the app lists your computers so you can sign one out. A computer unused for 60 days frees its place by itself.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Your licence is checked on your computer, without the internet. It renews itself whenever you are online, so only a computer kept offline for more than about 35 days needs to go online once.
Will my $3 go up later?
For the first 100 members, you keep $3 a month for as long as you stay subscribed, even after the price for new members rises. Or pay $100 once for a lifetime licence and never pay again.
Can I cancel?
Yes, at any time, from the Account section of the app.
We are a firm. Do we each need a subscription?
No. The Practices plan covers up to 15 computers in one office for one price.
