Why run it on your own computer

Cloud AI like ChatGPT is great for public research or heavy coding. Noodle is built for the work you wouldn’t paste into a public website: client contracts, financial spreadsheets, medical letters, and personal notes.

  1. 01

    Your files never leave the room.

    Nothing you type or open is ever uploaded. There is nothing to leak, no corporate training on your documents, and nobody else’s privacy policy to take on faith.

  2. 02

    Never get cut off mid-project.

    Good writing takes rewording and retrying. Cloud services throttle you or lock you out after a few dozen messages. On your own computer, the hundredth try is as free as the first.

  3. 03

    Smart enough for 90% of daily work.

    You don’t need a massive data centre to draft a letter, summarise a 40-page PDF, or spot a clause in a contract. Today’s compact models handle everyday work well.

  4. 04

    They set the terms. You live with them.

    Cloud AI companies decide the price, the limits and which model you get, and change them when it suits them. On your computer nothing changes unless you change it.

  5. 05

    You are not renting a model. You own the machine.

    A model is just a file. When a better one comes out, download it: same computer, no new contract. The one you have never changes or disappears unless you decide it should.

  6. 06

    Every year, more of it fits on your PC.

    A few years ago a home computer could run little that was useful. Today an ordinary graphics card runs models that handle most everyday work, and every new release moves more across the line.
